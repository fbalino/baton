    Paper Character 20 × 30 30 × 40 40 × 60

      Turnaround: 3 days to 20 sheets, 5 days to 60, 9 beyond. A deckled edge adds two days, 40 × 60 adds one.

      House terms

      These apply the same way to an order placed by a person and one placed by an agent.

      • Print legs only. We do not bind and we do not deliver.
      • Quantity, size and paper are declared before a press day is held.
      • A proof is approved before the slot, and re-issued if the specification changes.
      • Runs to 400 sheets. Larger jobs are split across two press days.
      • A held slot is released if the leg is not signed within 48 hours.
