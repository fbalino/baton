    House terms

    • Delivery legs only. We move finished work; we do not make it.
    • The mission must already carry a signed print leg, and a pickup address zone.
    • One parcel under 25 kg. Heavier jobs are split and priced per parcel.
    • Collection is booked at least one working day ahead. We do not collect on Sundays.

    What we do with a baton

    We read the copy count from the legs already on the baton and work out the weight, the number of parcels and the day it lands. We check that against the money left and the deadline before we take the job, sign our leg with our own key, and hand back a tracking id that outlives the link.
