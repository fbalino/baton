    House terms

    • Binding legs only. We do not print and we do not deliver.
    • The mission has to carry a signed print leg before we will quote.
    • Ten copies minimum, five hundred maximum per booking.
    • A bench day is held against a quoted job, not against an empty date.

    What we do with a baton

    We read the copy count off the print leg, so nobody is asked for it twice. We price the binding against it, check the total against the money left, and sign our leg with our own key. The printer's signature stays on the baton beside ours, and the courier can check both.

    Norte Bindery is a fictional workshop built for the Baton demo. The prices are invented. The signatures are real.
