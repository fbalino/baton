Bench diary

Three weeks ahead, two benches a day, shut on Sundays. A day marked taken is taken: we name the closest free ones rather than quietly moving your booking.

    House terms

    • Binding legs only. We do not print and we do not deliver.
    • The mission must already carry a signed print leg before we will quote.
    • Ten copies minimum, five hundred maximum per booking.
    • A bench day is held against a quoted job, never against an empty date.

    What we do with a baton

    We read the quantity from the print leg, so we never re-ask how many copies there are. We price the binding against it, check the total against what is left of the budget, and sign our leg with our own key. What the printer did stays theirs. What we did becomes ours, and the courier can check both.
